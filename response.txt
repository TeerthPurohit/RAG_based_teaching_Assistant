Machine learning, often abbreviated as ML, is a powerful approach that gives us a suite of tools for building predictive models using data.

As Professor Balch explains in video 164 (21.0-29.0), what makes machine learning different is its data-centric way of creating these models. Essentially, the goal is to build a "model" that can take in observations and then provide a prediction or output.

To elaborate a bit, as discussed in video 165 (0.4-17.7), machine learning algorithms work by taking historical data and processing it to generate this model. Once the model is built, you can then feed new observations, like features of a stock, into it to get predictions, such as a future price (82.5-107.6).

So, in short, it's about using data to intelligently build something that can make predictions.
